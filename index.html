The nightly job that should not have been nightly

Three hours became real time.

A product-catalog cron fetched more than 100K records every night and took three to four hours to finish. I redesigned it as an event-driven SQS and EventBridge pipeline, built the infrastructure and application code, and published webhooks to subscribers as updates happened.

“Not my ticket” is not a useful system boundary.

My first on-call incident

The logs did not know each other.

A run of 504s led to a degraded microservice, but there was no correlation across the transaction. I traced it manually once, then built the correlation-ID library I wished I had—and rolled it across 15+ microservices and five backend platforms.

One ID. The whole transaction. Much less detective work.
